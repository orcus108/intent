For user sessions, ask people to perform their ordinary work. Record which tool they choose next time and why, including occasions when typing is easier.

For each useful observation, add a short evidence record:

- **Observed:** What the person actually did or said, with date/context; use an anonymized label.
- **Interpretation:** Why it may have been difficult; separate this from the observation.
- **Hypothesis:** What change might help and what would disconfirm it.
- **Experiment:** The smallest interaction to test and the existing workflow to compare against.
- **Result:** Time to a verified outcome, corrections, checking effort, and voluntary reuse, with sample size.
- **Decision:** Keep, revise, or discard, and why.
